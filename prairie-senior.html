    About Prairie Senior

    A home, with care close by

    It is an honor to care for our residents. We want every resident to live comfortably and enjoyably while receiving the care they need.

    Prairie Senior offers long-term care and rehabilitation from dedicated health care professionals. As part of Citizens Health, residents have access to our medical providers and the largest group of specialty clinics in northwest Kansas, right next door.

    Life at Prairie Senior

    Everything residents need, in one place

    Care plans adjust as health changes, and residents stay connected to friends, family, and the Colby community.

    Health & care

    • Medical and nursing care from staff who are there when you need them
    • Daily medication management
    • A personal care plan that adjusts with changes in health
    • Physical, occupational, and speech therapy
    • Routine dental care
    • Nutritious meals planned with a registered dietitian
    • Social workers for emotional support

    Everyday life

    • Personal rooms in a choice of sizes, with modern conveniences
    • Help making your room feel like home
    • Dining room and group gathering areas
    • Outdoor gathering areas and birds in the atrium
    • Chapel and religious services
    • Salon services
    • Pet therapy
    • Fitness activities for health, energy, and strength
    • Celebrations for birthdays, holidays, and special days

    Help with the details

    • Transportation to community activities, errands, and medical appointments
    • Laundry and housekeeping
    • Therapeutic safe-step tub
    • Memory support activities built around favorite pastimes
    • Timely updates for families

    Special Care Unit

    Memory care for Alzheimer's & dementia

    Social Services
    (785) 462-8295To coordinate care or ask questions

    Our Special Care Unit is home for residents with Alzheimer's disease or other dementias, with a higher level of care and safety in a comfortable, caring setting.

    Specially trained staff provide all of Prairie Senior's services, plus memory, communication, and emotional support and help reducing stress.

    What the Special Care Unit offers

    • Memory support that encourages mental activity and clarity
    • Validation therapy and other communication strategies that protect dignity and reduce frustration
    • Brain fitness activities matched to each resident's stage of illness
    • Care that adapts as the illness changes
    • A care program covering social time, nutrition, stress management, and physical fitness

    For families

    You're part of the care

    Caring for a loved one with a chronic or terminal illness can become more than a family can safely provide. We keep residents safe, comfortable, and engaged, and we keep you involved.

    Time with friends and family is essential to a resident's well-being. Visitors are welcome any time, to visit or share a meal, and we encourage celebrations and social events. We answer your questions, talk through care plans with you, and keep you updated.

    Things to talk with us about

    • Personalizing the roomWe'll help make it feel like home.
    • Getting to know your loved oneTell us about their history, family, and friends.
    • VisitsWe can help arrange visits and shared meals.
    • Problems or worriesOur social workers offer emotional support for residents and families.
    • SafetyLet us know about any safety concerns.
    • The moveSocial workers help residents and families through the transition.

    Paying for care

    Understanding the cost

    Financial questions
    (785) 462-8295

    Our social workers and business office staff help families prepare for, understand, and receive available long-term care assistance.

    Ways to pay

    Private fundsPrivate insuranceMedicaid
    • Cost depends on the level of care needed and the room you choose
    • Level of care comes from an assessment and may change as health changes
    • Medicaid helps people with limited financial resources. Some Medicaid residents still pay part of the cost, called a share of cost or resident liability, usually as a flat monthly amount
    • Over-the-counter medicines, salon services, and personal items are billed separately

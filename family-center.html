    Services

    Care for every stage of life

    As your medical home, we coordinate your preventive, acute, and chronic care, with diagnostic, rehab, inpatient, and specialty care on the same campus.

    Primary & preventive care

    Regular visits to keep you healthy and catch problems early.

    • Annual exams and screenings
    • Chronic disease management for diabetes, heart disease, COPD, asthma, depression, anxiety, and osteoporosis
    • Weight loss program with nutrition counseling and medical supervision
    • Hearing and vision screenings
    More about chronic disease care →

    Children & teens

    Care from one week after birth through the teen years.

    • Well-child visits, including KAN Be Healthy
    • School, sports, daycare, and camp physicals
    • Asthma and ear infections
    • Adolescent health and HPV vaccines
    More about children's health →

    Women's health

    Annual exams and care through every stage.

    • Pap and HPV testing
    • Breast exams and mammograms
    • Birth control
    • Bone density (DEXA)
    • Menopause and hormone therapy
    • STI testing
    Pregnancy & obstetrics → More about women's health →

    Men's health

    Screenings that help prevent heart disease and cancer.

    • Cholesterol, blood pressure, and blood sugar
    • Prostate health
    • Colorectal and skin cancer screening
    • Colonoscopy
    • Vasectomy

    Older adults

    Medicare wellness visits and care where you live.

    • Welcome to Medicare preventive visit
    • Annual wellness visit
    • Nursing home program at Prairie Senior, Deseret Health & Rehab at Colby, and Fairview Estates
    More about care for older adults →

    Immunizations

    Vaccines for children and adults, scheduled by dedicated staff.

    • Childhood vaccines, including the Vaccines for Children program
    • Flu, pneumonia, and Tdap
    • Travel vaccines
    Kansas immunization records → More about immunizations →

    Minor procedures

    Taken care of in the clinic, without a trip elsewhere.

    • Skin biopsies and removal of growths
    • Laceration and wound repair
    • Earwax removal

    Counseling

    Our clinic social worker helps with anxiety, depression, grief, stress, and family or parenting concerns.

    Meet Amanda Sowers, LSCSW →

    For employers

    Services that keep your workforce healthy and safe.

    • Pre-employment and DOT physicals
    • Physical Capacity Profile testing
    • Workers' compensation care
    • Drug testing
    • On-site flu shots and health screenings
    More about employer services →

    Pay online in a few minutes, with or without a MyChart account.

    Pay a bill online

    Other ways to pay

    • One-time bank withdrawal
    • Automatic recurring bank withdrawals
    • Online through MyChart
    • Debit or credit card by phone
    • In person or by mail

    How billing works

    • Bring your insurance card to every visit
    • We file claims with your insurance for you
    • After your insurance pays, we bill you for any remaining balance
    • Co-payments are due at the time of service

    Charges from physicians, anesthesia, pathology, or outside labs may be billed separately by those providers. See the insurance plans we work with.

    Payment plans

    Pay over time

    Questions
    (785) 460-5640

    If you can't pay in full at your visit, we'll set up monthly payments based on your total balance.

    Your balancePaid in full within
    $50 or lessDue in full
    $51 to $2493 months
    $250 to $5006 months
    $501 to $7509 months
    $751 to $1,00012 months
    $1,001 or more16 months, after meeting with a Patient Financial Advisor
    • Self-pay patients who can't pay in full pay at least $50 at the visit
    • Uninsured patients receive a 20% discount, excluding aesthetic, cosmetic, and elective procedures
    • Regular monthly payments keep your account current and out of collections
    • Fill out a Payment Arrangement Agreement to get started

    Financial assistance

    Help paying for care

    Patient Financial Advisor
    (785) 460-5640

    As a non-profit, Citizens Health offers financial assistance to patients who are uninsured, underinsured, not eligible for a government program, or otherwise unable to pay for emergency or other medically necessary care.

    Eligibility is based on your financial situation. We never turn away patients who need emergency care, including those who qualify for financial or government assistance.

    How to apply

    1. Read the plain language summaryA short overview of the program and who qualifies.
    2. Fill out the applicationAvailable in English and Spanish.
    3. Return it to Patient Financial ServicesCall (785) 460-5640 with any questions.

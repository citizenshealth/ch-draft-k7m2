    Testing

    Heart and lung tests

    • EKGRecords your heart's rhythm
    • Holter and event monitorsTrack your heart rhythm at home
    • Cardiac stress test (GXT)Checks your heart while you walk on a treadmill
    • Pulmonary function testingMeasures how well your lungs work, including diffusion studies and plethysmography
    • Arterial blood gasMeasures oxygen and carbon dioxide in your blood
    • Six-minute walk testChecks oxygen levels while you walk
    • Overnight oximetryTracks oxygen levels while you sleep
    • Home sleep testingScreens for sleep apnea at home

    Our story

    Quality care, close to home

    Our goal is to be the first choice for health care in our region, and to earn that through collaboration and compassion.

    Our team of doctors, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, nurses, technicians, therapists, and support staff provides emergency care, inpatient and maternity care, surgery, rehabilitation, primary care, and long-term care. Our Specialty Clinic brings physicians from across the region to Colby, so you can see a specialist without a long drive.

    Today, our new hospital and Family Center for Healthcare share one campus at 1525 S. Franklin Ave., with Prairie Senior next door.

    Common questions

    Answers to what people ask most

    Citizens Medical Center

    What should I bring for a hospital stay?
    • Insurance card and photo ID
    • Glasses or contacts with case and solution, hearing aids, and dentures
    • Comfortable clothes and shoes
    • A list of all your medicines, including over-the-counter medicines, inhalers, eye drops, vitamins, and supplements
    • Your medical history, allergies, surgeries, and immunizations (especially tetanus, flu, and pneumonia)
    • Your CPAP, if you use one
    • A copy of your advance directive or living will
    • An emergency contact's name and number, and a list of questions for your providers
    What should I leave at home?
    Jewelry, large amounts of cash, credit cards, and anything sentimental or irreplaceable.
    When can I visit a patient?
    See visiting hours and guidelines. For the Maternity Suites, see their visiting guidelines.

    Family Center for Healthcare

    What should I bring to my appointment?
    Your photo ID, insurance card, and medicines or a current medication list. Arrive 15 minutes early so we can update your information. A parent or legal guardian should come with children under 18.
    I'm switching providers. What do I need to do?
    Have your medical records sent to us before your first visit, and tell us about any changes to your medical history, allergies, or surgeries.
    What if I have a medical question after hours?
    Established patients can reach an on-call provider for urgent questions that can't wait. See after-hours care. For refills, test results, and appointments, use MyChart. For an emergency, call 911.

    Prairie Senior

    How do I schedule a tour?
    Call Social Services at (785) 462-8295.
    What are the accommodations like?
    Prairie Senior has 60 beds: 34 long-term care, 12 in our Special Care Unit, and 14 residential care. Residents get the level of care they need now, with more care available later as needs change.

    Citizens Foundation

    Are gifts tax deductible?
    Yes. Citizens Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
    Can you help with a planned gift?
    Yes. Jen Schoenfeld, our Foundation Executive Director, will work with you, your attorney, and your financial planner. Call (785) 460-1264, email jschoenfeld@cmciks.com, or see ways to give.

    Have a question we didn't answer?

    Call our main line at (785) 462-7511, or send us a message.

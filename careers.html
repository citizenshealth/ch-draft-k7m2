    Students & education

    Learn with us

    Education
    education@cmciks.comObservation, clinical, and class questions

    Education is part of health care. We offer observation and clinical experiences for students of every level and discipline.

    Students interested in an observation or clinical rotation can email our education team. Nursing students can also apply for open student positions, and Citizens Foundation offers scholarships for health care students.

    Certification classes

    Taught by our own instructors and open to staff and community health care professionals.

    • ACLSAdvanced Cardiac Life Support. Builds on BLS, with a focus on high-quality CPR and teamwork.
    • PALSPediatric Advanced Life Support. Simulated pediatric emergencies covering assessment, resuscitation, and teamwork.
    • Rhythms & PharmacologyAn introduction to ACLS and PALS: heart anatomy, ECG rhythms, and medications. Good for new graduates or a refresher.
    • TNCC, 8th EditionTrauma Nursing Core Course for emergency nurses. One and a half days, 18.3 CNE contact hours.
    • S.T.A.B.L.E.Stabilizing sick newborns after resuscitation and before transport.

    Life in Colby

    The Oasis on the Plains

    Colby is a town of about 5,000 in Thomas County, with Citizens Health serving patients from nine counties.

    Families here enjoy affordable housing, strong public schools, outdoor recreation, and the shops, restaurants, and services of a regional hub, along with Colby Community College.

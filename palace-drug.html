    Services

    What we can help with

    Prescriptions

    New prescriptions and refills, sent by your Citizens Health provider or any provider you see.

    Talk with a pharmacist

    Questions about a new medicine, side effects, or how your medicines work together.

    Over-the-counter care

    Help choosing cold, allergy, pain, and first aid products that are safe with your prescriptions.

    Medicare & Medicaid

    We accept Medicare, Medicaid, and most major insurance plans.

    Care team connection

    As part of Citizens Health, we can work directly with your providers at Citizens Medical Center and Family Center.

    Cards & gifts

    Greeting cards and gifts for birthdays, holidays, and get-well wishes.

    Refills

    Refill a prescription

    Pharmacy
    (785) 460-7507

    Call us with your prescription number, or ask your provider to send a new prescription when you're out of refills.

    1. Find your prescription numberIt's printed on the label of your bottle.
    2. Call (785) 460-7507Give us your name, date of birth, and prescription number.
    3. Pick up at the counterWe'll let you know when it's ready.

    No refills left? We'll contact your provider for you.

    Transfers

    Move your prescriptions to Palace

    Switching pharmacies is easy. Tell us where your prescriptions are filled now, and we'll handle the transfer.

    1. Call us or stop byBring your prescription bottles if you have them.
    2. Tell us your current pharmacyWe'll contact them and transfer your prescriptions.
    3. We'll call when it's readyFuture refills will come from Palace.

    Insurance & payment

    Paying for your prescriptions

    We accept Medicare, Medicaid, and most major insurance plans. Bring your insurance card the first time you fill a prescription with us.

    Not sure if your plan is accepted, or worried about the cost of a medicine? Call us. Our pharmacists can look for lower-cost options and talk with your provider.

    Financial assistance at Citizens Health

    Our new location

    Coming to the Citizens Health campus

    Early next year, Palace Drug Store will move into our main campus at 1525 S. Franklin Ave.

    One stop for your careSee your provider, get lab work or imaging, and pick up your prescriptions in the same building. Until the move, we're open at 460 N. Franklin Ave. downtown.
